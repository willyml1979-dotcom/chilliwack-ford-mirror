Experience the 2027 Ford Transit Cargo Van in Oxford White, designed for dependable performance and versatile utility with advanced safety and technology features.

Powered by a robust 3.5L EcoBoost V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic overdrive transmission featuring SelectShift®, this all-wheel-drive van offers confident handling and efficient power delivery. The 3.73 limited-slip axle ratio enhances traction, making it ideal for demanding road conditions. Inside, the durable dark Palazzo grey vinyl bucket seats provide comfort with 2-way manual adjustments and armrests for the driver, ensuring support during long hauls. The interior flooring features front and rear vinyl with wheel well liners, built to withstand heavy use.

Safety is prioritized with a comprehensive suite including automatic headlights, automatic high beams, lane departure warning, lane keeping assist, front collision mitigation, brake assist, and multiple airbags. Stability and traction control systems further enhance security on the road. Convenience features such as keyless entry and start, back-up camera, rear parking aid, and rain-sensing wipers streamline daily operation. The van is equipped with Bluetooth connectivity, steering wheel audio controls, and a WiFi hotspot to keep you connected while on the move. Steel wheels with silver hubcaps and exposed lug nuts complete the rugged exterior, ready to support your business needs.
