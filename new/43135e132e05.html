Experience the 2027 Ford Explorer Active with 200A Package: a versatile 4WD SUV designed for confident driving and advanced safety in a striking Carbonized Grey Metallic finish.

This new Ford Explorer features a turbocharged 2.3L EcoBoost 4-cylinder engine paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission, delivering responsive power and efficient performance. The 4x4 drivetrain ensures capability across diverse terrains, making it ideal for drivers seeking adventure or daily versatility. Seating for six includes comfortable bucket seats and a convenient third-row, while the power driver seat enhances comfort on longer journeys.

Safety is paramount with a comprehensive suite of features including Blind Spot Monitor, Front Collision Mitigation, Lane Departure Warning, Lane Keeping Assist, Rear Collision Mitigation, and Cross-Traffic Alert. Additional protections like Driver Monitoring, Brake Assist, Stability Control, and multiple airbags provide peace of mind for every trip.

The Explorer is equipped with modern conveniences such as Adaptive Cruise Control with Steering Assist, Keyless Entry and Start, Heated Mirrors, LED Headlights, and a Back-Up Camera complemented by Rear Parking Aid. Aluminum wheels and all-season tires round out the rugged yet refined exterior, complete with a sporty rear spoiler and tow hitch for added utility. Inside, the Ultra Dark Space Grey cabin offers multi-zone climate control and Bluetooth connectivity to keep you comfortable and connected.
