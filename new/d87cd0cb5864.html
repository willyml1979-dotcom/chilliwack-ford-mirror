Experience confident driving with the 2027 Ford Explorer Active, featuring advanced safety, powerful AWD, and refined comfort for every journey in Agate Black Metallic.

The 2027 Ford Explorer Active combines robust performance with thoughtful design, powered by a 2.3L EcoBoost® 4-cylinder engine with auto start-stop technology paired to a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission. Its all-wheel-drive system ensures traction and control across diverse Canadian road conditions. The exterior boasts 20-inch carbonized grey-painted aluminum wheels with all-season tires, complemented by LED headlights, LED fog lamps, and signature lighting framed by silver-painted skid plates for a rugged yet sophisticated look.

Inside, the Ultra Dark Space Grey cabin offers heated front bucket seats with driver lumbar adjustment and a power driver seat for tailored comfort. The Active Comfort Package enhances convenience with a heated steering wheel, second-row HVAC controls, and remote start system, perfect for chilly mornings. Safety is paramount with features including blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, cross-traffic alert, and a comprehensive airbag system. Additional amenities such as a back-up camera, adaptive cruise control with steering assist, keyless entry and start, power liftgate, and rear parking aid provide ease and confidence on every drive. The sleek roof design omits rails for a streamlined profile, while floor liners replace standard carpet mats for durability. This Explorer Active is built to meet the demands of drivers seeking capability and comfort in a versatile SUV.
