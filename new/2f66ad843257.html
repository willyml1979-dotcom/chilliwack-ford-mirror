Experience commanding presence and advanced luxury with the 2027 Ford Expedition MAX Platinum in striking Agate Black Metallic, designed for those who demand capability and refinement.

Powered by a robust 3.5L EcoBoost® V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift® capability, this 4x4 SUV delivers confident performance on any terrain. The STEALTH Appearance Package enhances its bold look with black platinum badges, ebony trim headlamps, carbon black front fascia, and 22-inch Magnetite painted aluminum wheels wrapped in P275/50R22 all-season tires. Power retractable running boards and power-folding sideview mirrors with memory and auto-dimming add convenience and style.

Inside, enjoy premium comfort with leather bucket seats featuring heated and cooled front seats, heated rear seats, and power-adjustable driver and passenger seats with lumbar support and memory functions. The Driver’s Package includes a power tilt/telescoping steering column with memory, wireless charging pad, and a panoramic Vista roof with power shade that fills the cabin with natural light.

Safety is comprehensive with adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, and a suite of airbags. The Aerial View Display system, back-up camera, and rear parking aid enhance confidence in every manoeuvre. Premium B&O Play Unleashed sound system with 22 speakers offers an immersive audio experience tailored to your mood.

This Expedition MAX Platinum is built for those who seek power, sophistication, and advanced technology in a full-size SUV.
