Experience the 2027 Ford Super Duty F-350 XLT Crew Cab with powerful 7.3L V8 gas engine, advanced 4x4 drivetrain, and rugged FX4 Off-Road Package for serious work and off-road capability.

Built for demanding tasks, this Super Duty features a robust 7.3L 2V DEVCT NA PFI V8 engine with gaseous engine prep, including hardened intake valves and bi-fuel manifold, ensuring durability and versatility. The 10-speed automatic transmission with selectable drive modes and locking limited-slip differential delivers smooth power and control across all terrains. The FX4 Off-Road Package enhances performance with rock crawl drive mode, hill descent control, premium off-road shock absorbers, and skid plates protecting the transfer case and fuel tank, making it ready for tough off-road conditions.

The crew cab offers spacious seating with driver adjustable lumbar support and a pass-through rear bench seat, complemented by all-weather floor mats designed for durability without carpet. Safety is comprehensive with multiple airbags, stability and traction control, brake assist, tow hooks, and integrated turn signal mirrors. Convenience features include a back-up camera, Bluetooth connectivity, keyless entry, heated mirrors, and six overhead upfitter switches for custom accessories. The exterior in Oxford White includes running boards, aluminum wheels, and a conventional spare tire, making this truck as functional as it is tough.
