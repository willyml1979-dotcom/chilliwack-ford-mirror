Experience the 2027 Ford Explorer Tremor in Vapor Blue Metallic, combining rugged capability with advanced technology and comfort for every Canadian adventure.

This new Ford Explorer Tremor is powered by a 2.3L EcoBoost® turbocharged 4-cylinder engine featuring Auto Start-Stop Technology, paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission and all-wheel drive for confident handling on any terrain. Designed for versatility, it comes equipped with all-terrain tires front and rear, tow hooks, and a tow hitch, making it ready for trail or tow.

Inside, the Onyx interior offers premium synthetic bucket seats with heated and cooled front seats, heated rear seats, and power-adjustable driver and passenger seats with lumbar support for long drives in comfort. The third-row seating adds flexibility for family or gear. The leather steering wheel is heated and features audio controls, while the multi-zone climate control ensures comfort for all passengers.

Safety is comprehensive with Blind Spot Monitor, Cross-Traffic Alert, Lane Keeping Assist, Front and Rear Collision Mitigation, and an Aerial View Display System to enhance awareness. Additional convenience comes from a power liftgate, remote engine start, keyless entry and start, and a premium sound system with Bluetooth connectivity.

The Explorer Tremor balances rugged capability with refined features, ideal for drivers seeking adventure without compromise.
