Discover the 2027 Ford Transit Passenger Van XL in Oxford White, designed for versatile passenger transport with advanced safety and convenience features.

Powered by a robust 3.5L EcoBoost V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic overdrive transmission featuring SelectShift®, this AWD Transit delivers confident performance and control. Dual rear wheels and a locking limited-slip differential enhance stability and traction in diverse driving conditions. The exterior is equipped with passenger running boards covering the B-C pillar for easy entry and exit, complemented by front and rear all-season tires and fog lamps integrated into the front fascia for improved visibility.

Inside, the Dark Palazzo grey cloth bucket seats provide comfort with 2-way manual adjustments, lumbar support for the driver, adjustable headrests, and armrests for both front occupants. Seating includes a rear bench and a third-row seat, accommodating multiple passengers comfortably. The cabin benefits from rear A/C and privacy glass for passenger comfort and discretion.

Safety is paramount with Ford Co-Pilot360 Assist 2.0, including adaptive cruise control with predictive speed assist, a 360-degree camera system, Blind Spot Information System (BLIS), cross-traffic alert, lane keeping assist, automatic headlights with high beams, and multiple airbags. Additional features like power folding heated mirrors with turn signals, rain-sensing wipers, and a tire pressure monitor enhance convenience and safety on every journey.
