Powered by a robust 6.7L High Output Power Stroke® V8 Turbo Diesel engine paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission, this 4x4 Super Duty delivers exceptional torque and towing strength. The FX4 Off-Road Package includes rock crawl drive mode, hill descent control, premium off-road shock absorbers, and skid plates for fuel tank and transfer case protection, ensuring confidence on challenging terrain. Inside, the Carhartt Package adds durable style with Carhartt-themed door trim, fender vents, all-weather floor mats, and a wrapped steering wheel with Carhartt stitching. The XLT Premium Package upgrades comfort and technology with heated front seats, a 12-inch SYNC 4 touchscreen, B&O Sound System with eight speakers and subwoofer, remote start, and a 360-degree camera system featuring trailer coverage and reverse brake assist.

Additional rugged touches include 20-inch machined and painted wheels with LT275/65Rx20E BSW all-terrain tires, LED roof marker lamps, tough spray-in bedliner with Carhartt logo, and six upfitter switches for custom accessory control. This Super Duty is ready to tackle any job with strength, technology, and style.
