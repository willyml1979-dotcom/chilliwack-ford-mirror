Experience the 2027 Ford Transit Passenger Van XLT in striking Agate Black Metallic, designed for versatile passenger transport with advanced safety and comfort features.

Powered by a robust 3.5L EcoBoost V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic overdrive transmission and SelectShift®, this AWD van delivers confident handling and performance. Dual rear wheels and a locking limited-slip differential enhance stability and traction, ideal for varying road conditions. The heavy-duty trailer tow package includes a frame-mounted hitch receiver and tow/haul mode with trailer wiring provisions, perfect for hauling needs.

Inside, Dark Palazzo grey cloth heated bucket seats provide comfort with two-way manual adjustments, lumbar support, and armrests for driver and front passenger. The third-row seating and rear bench accommodate multiple passengers comfortably. Practical vinyl floor coverings with wheel well liners ensure durability for heavy use.

Safety is paramount with features such as automatic high beams, blind spot monitoring, cross-traffic alert, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, and a comprehensive airbag system. The aerial view display system and rear parking aid enhance visibility during manoeuvres, complemented by power folding heated mirrors and HID headlights for improved road illumination.

Additional conveniences include power third passenger door, passenger running boards, rain-sensing wipers, keyless entry and start, Bluetooth connectivity, satellite radio, WiFi hotspot, and climate control with rear A/C, making this Transit a reliable and comfortable choice for passenger transport.
