Power, capability, and advanced safety converge in the 2027 Ford Super Duty F-350 SRW XLT Crew Cab 4x4 Diesel Truck, built for demanding work and rugged terrain.

This new Super Duty F-350 SRW features a robust 6.7L turbocharged 8-cylinder diesel engine paired with a Torqshift 10-speed automatic transmission, delivering exceptional torque and power for heavy towing and hauling. The 4x4 drivetrain with locking/limited slip differential ensures superior traction and control in challenging conditions. The crew cab offers spacious seating for five, including a rear bench seat with pass-through access and driver adjustable lumbar support for comfort during long drives.

Safety is paramount with integrated features such as automatic headlights, brake assist, multiple airbags including front side and rear head airbags, stability and traction control, tow hooks, and child safety locks. The truck is equipped with a tow hitch and 4-wheel disc brakes with ABS for confident stopping power.

Convenience and connectivity come standard with keyless entry, Bluetooth, back-up camera, cruise control, heated mirrors with integrated turn signals, steering wheel audio controls, and a WiFi hotspot. The exterior in Oxford White complements the rugged design, while the gray interior balances durability and comfort. This Ford Super Duty F-350 SRW XLT is engineered to meet the needs of professionals who demand strength, reliability, and advanced features on every job.
