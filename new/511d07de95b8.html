Experience commanding capability and refined comfort with the 2027 Ford Explorer Tremor in striking Avalanche Grey, designed for adventurous drivers seeking performance and advanced technology.

Powered by a robust 3.0L EcoBoost® V6 engine with Auto Start-Stop technology paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission and all-wheel drive, this Explorer Tremor delivers confident handling on all terrains. The rugged all-terrain tires and tow hitch enhance its readiness for outdoor excursions, while the locking limited-slip differential ensures traction in challenging conditions.

Inside, the Onyx interior features premium synthetic bucket seats with power adjustments, heated and cooled front seats, heated rear seats, and front multi-contour massaging seats with active motion for exceptional comfort on long drives. Seat memory and lumbar adjustments for both driver and passenger add personalized convenience. The 3rd-row power-fold seats fold flat to maximize cargo space.

Technology enthusiasts will appreciate the Bang & Olufsen 980-watt premium sound system with 14 speakers, panoramic Vista Roof with power shade, and BlueCruise hands-free highway driving system with automatic lane changes and lane keeping assist. Safety is paramount with blind spot monitoring, front collision mitigation, lane departure warning, cross-traffic alert, and a comprehensive airbag system.

Additional features include LED headlights, rain-sensing wipers, power-folding heated mirrors with memory, open-on-approach liftgate, and ambient interior lighting—perfect for those who demand versatility and sophistication in their SUV.
